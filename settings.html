03About Zone

v2.0

Zone

Personal utilities · D1-backed

A set of small, ordinary tools for keeping track of a life: what you spent, what you meant to do, what you watched, what you wrote down. It runs in the browser and stores its records in your own Cloudflare D1 database — no account, no third-party trackers.

There is no sync and no analytics. If the browser's site data is cleared, the workspace goes with it — the export in Admin is the honest backup.
