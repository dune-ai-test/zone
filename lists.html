How a list behaves

  1. 01
    A line is the thing.

    Steps live beneath it, so the small pieces never float away from their parent.

  2. 02
    The last step closes the line.

    Tick every step and the parent follows. Tick the parent and the whole line is crossed at once.

  3. 03
    Move the important things up.

    Use the arrows on a line to put the next thing at the top of the page.
