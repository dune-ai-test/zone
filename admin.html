How the review works

A small, legible system.

01

Lands live

An add, edit, or delete applies to the tool immediately, so the person at the front desk can keep working.

02

Waits in Activity

Manual review files the before and after under Activity as pending. Nothing is hidden and nothing is silently lost.

03

Approve or restore

Approve keeps the change. Reject or roll back writes the previous version back and leaves a dated restore entry.
